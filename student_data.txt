[EXAM SCHEDULE]
The semester examination schedule is published by the examination cell.
Students should check the latest examination notice for dates, rooms, and
reporting times. Contact the examination cell at examcell@example.edu for
clarifications.

[STUDENT GUIDELINES]
Students must carry their identity card, follow the academic integrity policy,
arrive at least  fifteen minutes before an examination, and follow instructions
from faculty members. Keep institutional notices and personal records private.

[LIBRARY]
The library is open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Students can
borrow up to three books for fourteen days. Use the library desk for renewals
and lost-book questions.

[FEES]
Fee-payment dates are published by the accounts office. Students should keep
their payment receipt and contact accounts@example.edu if a payment is not
reflected in the student portal.

[ATTENDANCE]
Students should maintain the attendance percentage required by their
institution and contact their department office when medical or approved
leave documentation is needed.
